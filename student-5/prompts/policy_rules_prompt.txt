OmniTech Policy Rules

PC Hardware:
- If the PC hardware was damaged or defective upon arrival, the claim should be approved
- If the PC hardware develops a fault during normal use and the fault was not caused by the customer, the claim should be approved
- If the PC hardware was physically damaged by the customer, the claim should be rejected
- If the PC hardware was modified, incorrectly installed, or damaged due to improper use by the customer, the claim should be rejected

Home Appliances:
- If the home appliance was damaged or defective upon arrival, the claim should be approved
- If the home appliance stops working during normal use and the fault was not caused by the customer, the claim should be approved
- If the home appliance was physically damaged by the customer, the claim should be rejected
- If the home appliance was damaged due to improper use, misuse, or incorrect installation by the customer, the claim should be rejected
