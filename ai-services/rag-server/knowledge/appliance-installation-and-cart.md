# Appliance Installation, Power and Cart Guide

Student 4 (Cart and Order Processing) knowledge for grounding the cart AI
helper's power, clearance, delivery and accessory advice.

## Power Points and 10A Circuits
A standard Australian power point is 10A at 240V, which supplies at most
2400W. Appliances with a 10A plug can go in any standard power point. A 15A
plug has a wider earth pin and only fits a 15A power point. Built-in
cooktops and ovens over 3600W are hardwired on their own 32A circuit by a
licensed electrician - never plug them into a power board.

## Running Kitchen Appliances Together
Heating appliances such as induction cooktops, microwaves, air fryers and
kettles each draw 1000W or more. Two of them on one power point or double
adaptor can go over the 2400W limit and trip the circuit breaker. Plug each
heating appliance into its own power point, and never use a double adaptor
or power board for them.

## Fridge Ventilation Clearance
Fridges need air space so the compressor can release heat. Follow the
clearance listed for your model (rear, side and top). Too little space makes
the fridge use more power and run louder. Let a new fridge stand upright for
at least 2 hours after delivery before switching it on.

## Microwave and Air Fryer Clearance
Benchtop microwaves and air fryers blow out hot air. Keep the clearance
listed for the model around the vents, keep them away from walls and
overhead cupboards, and do not cover the vents while cooking.

## Delivery of Large and Heavy Appliances
Items over 30kg are delivered by a two-person team. Measure doorways,
hallways and stairs before checkout - the appliance depth plus packaging must
fit through every door on the way. Standard delivery places the item in the
room of your choice; old-appliance removal must be requested at checkout.

## Out-of-Stock Items in the Cart
Out-of-stock items can still be ordered. They are placed on backorder and
ship separately as soon as new stock arrives, and the customer is emailed a
new delivery estimate. Payment for the whole order is taken at checkout.

## Suggested Accessories
Fridges with a water dispenser need a replacement water filter every 6
months. Induction cooktops only work with induction-ready (magnetic)
cookware. Air fryers work best with parchment liners for easy cleaning.
Suggest at most one accessory per appliance, and only accessories that match
items in the cart.
