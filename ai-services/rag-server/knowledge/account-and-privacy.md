# Account and Privacy

## Creating an Account
Customers can create an account with an email address and password, or
continue as a guest for a single order. Guest orders can be linked to an
account later using the order confirmation email.

## Data We Collect
We store the information needed to fulfil orders and provide support:
contact details, shipping addresses, order history, and saved product
recommendations from the AI consultant. We do not sell customer data to
third parties.

## Managing Your Data
Customers can update or delete their account details from account settings
at any time. Deleting an account removes stored personal details but retains
anonymised order records for accounting purposes.
