# Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty Coverage
All new products sold on OmniTech Marketplace come with a minimum 12-month
manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Laptops
and monitors include a 24-month warranty by default.

## What's Covered
Hardware failures that occur under normal use, DOA (dead-on-arrival) units
reported within 14 days of delivery, and battery capacity dropping below 80%
within the warranty period for devices with a rated battery life.

## What's Not Covered
Accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repairs or modifications,
cosmetic wear, and lost or stolen items are not covered under the standard
warranty. Extended accidental-damage protection can be purchased separately
at checkout.

## Filing a Warranty Claim
Open a claim from the product's order page and describe the fault. Most
claims are triaged within 2 business days; depending on the product, we
offer a repair, replacement, or refund.
