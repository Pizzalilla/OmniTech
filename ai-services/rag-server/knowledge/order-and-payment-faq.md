# Orders and Payment FAQ

## Accepted Payment Methods
We accept major credit and debit cards, and PayPal. Payment is processed at
checkout and orders are only confirmed once payment succeeds.

## Tracking an Order
Once an order ships, a tracking link is emailed and also shown under "Past
Orders" in your account. Delivery estimates are based on the shipping option
chosen at checkout.

## Changing or Cancelling an Order
Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of being placed by
contacting support, after which the order may already be picked for shipping
and can no longer be modified - use the standard return process instead.

## Discounts and Promo Codes
Promo codes are entered at checkout and apply to eligible items only; only
one code can be used per order.
