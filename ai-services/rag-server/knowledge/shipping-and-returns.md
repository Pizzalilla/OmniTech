# Shipping and Returns

## Shipping Options
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over $75; orders
under $75 have a flat $6.99 fee. Express shipping (1-2 business days) costs
$14.99 regardless of order size. We currently only ship within Australia.

## Return Window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided
they are unused and in their original packaging. Opened software licenses and
gift cards are not eligible for return.

## How to Start a Return
Start a return from the "Past Orders" section of your account, or contact
support with your order number. Once approved, we email a prepaid return
label - return shipping is free for defective items and $5.99 for
change-of-mind returns.

## Refund Timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of
us receiving the returned item.
